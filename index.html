Mountain View Province (MVP), formerly known as Mountain View Palace, is a private student hostel solution, where nature and affluence speak at its peak. It is powered by "Afri-Kard Integrated Solutions International"(one of the Promoters of Hall of Malls Inc.) strategically located in Diplomatic Zone, Katampe Extension Abuja. It is an off-campus hostel accomodation.
Simply unbeatable hostel solution, creating a home away from home experience for both long and short term students.
At MVP, we pride ourselves on top-notch facilities, security, and services. The awe-inspiring Mountain View provides a quiet and serene environment for relaxation and creativity while pursuing academic excellence.
MVP seeks to bridge the gap in hostel solution for students in the Nile University of Nigeria, and Baze University Abuja.
Our students' success is our pride. MVP has carefully packaged a bouquet of services to bridge the needs of students.
